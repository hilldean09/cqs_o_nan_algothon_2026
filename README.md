# CQS O(NaN) Algothon 2026
Our team's submission for the 2026 Algothon, an algorithmic trading competition run by the UNSW Fintech Society and Susquehanna.

# About Us
Our team is "CQS O(NaN)" and consists of four members of the Curtin Quant Society (CQS) committee.

Our team nane is a double entendre. 

The first, and likely most obvious interpretation being a play on the standard big-O notation for algorithmic asymptotic complexity with the limiting function havong been replaced ny "NaN", the Not-A-Number value returned by "illegal" arithmetic operations in computing. 

The second meaning being related to the mathematic field of group theory where there exists the sporadic simple group O'Nan. Simple sporadic groups are of notable interest in group theory, additionally, the O'Nan group is even more unique, being known as a "pariah", which are few even amongst the rare sporadic simple groups.
